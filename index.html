A study on language fairness in AI

Speaking বাংলা to AI costs 5× more.

AI models read text in small pieces called tokens, and every token is billed. The same sentence in Bangla is cut into far more pieces than in English, so Bangla speakers pay more, wait longer, and fit less into the model's memory.

Why it matters

১

Higher cost

AI APIs bill per token. If a Bangla message needs 5× the tokens, a Bangladeshi startup pays 5× for the same feature.

২

Slower replies

Models write one token at a time. More tokens for the same answer means people writing in Bangla wait longer.

৩

Less memory

A model's context window is measured in tokens. Bangla fills it faster, so the model can read less of your document.

So why does it happen?

Tokenizers learn their vocabulary from training text that is mostly English, so common English words become a single token. Bangla is rare in that text, and every Bangla letter takes 3 bytes in UTF-8. The tokenizer often has no piece for it and falls back to raw bytes, splitting a single ক into two or three tokens. Newer tokenizers with larger, more multilingual vocabularies, like GPT-4o's, close much of the gap.
